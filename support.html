Frequently Asked Questions

How does ValueCam work?
Take a photo of any object and ValueCam identifies what you're looking at, then estimates its market value in your local currency based on comparable market prices. You can refine the estimate, unlock POWER Refine for a deeper valuation report, download a PDF, or share a branded card.
Is ValueCam free?
Yes! ValueCam is free to use. Standard valuations are unlimited; POWER Refine uses sponsored unlock content.
How accurate are the valuations?
Valuations are estimates based on comparable market knowledge. They provide a helpful price range but should not be considered professional appraisals.
What is a valuation report?
A downloadable PDF with your photos, price range, and written analysis — useful for selling, insurance, or your records. Tap the PDF option on the results screen after a valuation.
Why does the app need my location?
Location is used to provide region-specific pricing and display values in your local currency. You can deny location access and the app will still work.
Why does the app need camera access?
The camera is used to photograph items for valuation. You can also pick existing photos from your gallery instead.
Is my data stored anywhere?
Photos are sent for analysis in real time and are not stored on our servers. App preferences and scan statistics are stored locally on your device only.
How do I delete my data?
Simply uninstall the app. All data is stored on your device only.
