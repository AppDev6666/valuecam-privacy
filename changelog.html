Changelog

All notable user-facing changes to ValueCam.

Version 1.1.0 — Build 113 (July 2026 · pending App Store release)

Performance and polish update.

Faster experience

  • Quicker valuations — Optimized valuation engine for standard scans and refinements; premium POWER Refine uses a deeper analysis model when you need maximum detail.
  • Results revealed sooner — The processing screen hands you your valuation faster.
  • Faster app launch — The app opens immediately; sponsored content loads in the background instead of blocking the splash screen.
  • Smaller download — App icons and splash assets optimized for a leaner install and quicker first paint.

Better results screen

  • Read the full analysis — Long valuation write-ups are no longer cut off; tap Read more to expand and Show less to collapse.
  • POWER Refine retry — If the unlock video can't load, the button now offers a clear tap to retry instead of staying stuck on “Loading…”.

Reliability

  • Valuation cache persists — Recent valuations are remembered across app restarts (24-hour cache).
  • Clearer error messages — Service errors use plain language instead of technical setup hints.
  • Accessibility — Close and remove-photo buttons now have proper screen-reader labels.

Version 1.1.0 — Build 112 (July 2026 · live on the App Store)

Major feature release.

New in 1.1

Refine your estimate

Answer follow-up questions or add your own details to sharpen the price range — refine as many times as you like.

POWER Refine

Unlock a deeper premium analysis. Add close-up photos, labels, or extra angles for a more detailed valuation.

Download PDF report

Save a full report with your photos, price range, and analysis — useful for selling, insurance, or your records.

Share your valuation

Send a branded valuation card to friends or post it on social media in one tap.

See on eBay

Jump straight to sold listings for similar items to compare real market prices.

Improvements

  • Faster, more accurate valuations — Upgraded valuation engine with improved reliability on slow connections (fewer timeouts, automatic retries).
  • Refreshed interface — Cleaner layout with better-organized screens.
  • Clear sponsored content — All ads are labeled “Sponsored”, visually separated from app buttons, and the processing screen includes an exit button so you are never stuck on an ad screen.
